The approach

स्तोत्रम्

Taught the Gurukula way: sound first, then script, then grammar, then texts.

A stotra is meant to be chanted every day, so it’s worth learning it right, once and for all.

I teach line by line, with clean pronunciation and the right rhythm of the metre, and I explain the meaning so the words never become just sounds.

Method

How I teach stotras

01

Line by line

You hear the line, you repeat it, I correct it. We build the stotra a few lines at a time.

02

Metre and rhythm

Every stotra has its chandas. Chanting in the right metre makes it flow, and makes it easier to remember.

03

The meaning

Long compound names are opened up word by word, so you know whom you are praising and how.

04

Making it daily

We help you settle the stotra into your daily routine, so it stays with you.
