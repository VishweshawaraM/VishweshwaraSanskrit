How learning works

From the Acharya's voice to the student's ear.

01

Listen

Every sound is said clearly by the Acharya, exactly as it was received in the Gurukula.

02

Repeat

The student says it back, aloud and live. No recordings to copy, no guessing.

03

Correct

Pronunciation and rhythm are corrected gently, in the moment, before habits form.

04

Understand

Then the meaning opens, word by word, so every verse is understood and not only memorised.

Meet the Acharya

Trained in a Gurukula.
Sharing it with the world.

Acharya Vishweshwara M studied for more than seven years in a traditional Gurukula: Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda, the Bhagavad Gītā, Vedānta and Śaṅkara Bhāṣya. From 2018 to 2025 he lived and studied at Veda Vijnana Gurukulam in Bengaluru. He holds an M.A., and today teaches students and families in India, the USA, the UK, the UAE and Australia.

Questions

What people ask.

My child doesn't know Hindi or Kannada. Can they still join?

Yes. Classes are taught in English and Kannada, and Sanskrit is learned from the sounds up.

Are the classes live?

Yes. Every class is live with the Acharya, in a small batch or one-to-one.

Can adults and overseas students join?

Yes. The one-to-one Pathway is for any age, with times set to your time zone.

How is the Guru Dakshina decided?

It is shared personally with each family on WhatsApp.
