The approach

वेदान्तः

Taught the Gurukula way: sound first, then script, then grammar, then texts.

Vedanta needs questions, and questions need time. That’s why I teach it only one-to-one.

We read from the original texts in the tradition of Adi Shankaracharya. You can stop me anywhere and ask why, and we stay there until it’s clear.

Method

How I teach Vedanta

01

From the source

We read the Sanskrit text itself, with its traditional explanation, not someone’s summary of it.

02

Foundations before conclusions

What Vedanta is for, how we know anything (pramana), and the qualities a seeker needs (sadhana chatushtaya), before the big statements.

03

Your questions shape the class

Doubts are welcome. Working through them is how the teaching becomes yours.

04

Sanskrit alongside

Basic grammar and Tarka Sangraha are studied in parallel, so the texts open up more and more on their own.
