About the Acharya

AcharyaVishweshwara M.

I teach Sanskrit, the Veda and the Gita the way a Gurukula teaches. Seven years of traditional Gurukula study, 2018 to 2025. Today I teach children and adults across India, the USA, the UK, the UAE and Australia, live and online.

What I studied

Seven years,
step by step.

Sanskrit grammar

Pāṇini's grammar, studied the traditional way, so that any text can be opened and read.

Krishna Yajurveda

The Taittirīya recension, learnt by heart with every svara and akshara in its place.

Bhagavad Gītā

Verse by verse, with the classical commentary of Śaṅkara.

Advaita Vedānta

The Upaniṣads and Śaṅkara Bhāṣya, in the tradition of Shankaracharya.

How I teach

The way it was
taught to me.

01

Sound first

Every letter and svara is corrected live, from the first class. Clean pronunciation is the foundation of everything else.

02

Word by word

We read the original text and understand each word, so students never depend only on a translation.

03

Every voice heard

One-to-one, or small groups. I hear every student, every class, and correct what needs correcting.

04

Gurukula discipline

Regular practice, respect for the text, steady habits. Taught with warmth, never with fear.
