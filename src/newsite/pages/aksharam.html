Terms, licence & refunds

Will it work on my Mac?

Aksharam works on Apple Silicon (M1–M4) and Intel Macs with macOS 12 Monterey or later.

What happens when a pass ends?

The keyboard shows a gentle reminder in its menu and keeps working for 7 more days, so you are never cut off mid-sentence. Renew any time.

Is my typing private?

Yes. The keyboard never uses the internet. Nothing you type is recorded, saved or sent.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If it does not work on your Mac, message us within 7 days for help or a full refund.
