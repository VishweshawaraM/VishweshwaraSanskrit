Guru Dakshina

GuruDakshina.

In the Gurukula tradition, learning is not a product. The Guru Dakshina is an offering that supports the Acharya and honours the knowledge.

Offer Guru Dakshina How to pay

How it unfolds

Study. Sincerity. Offering.

अध्ययनम्

Adhyayana · study

Attend live classes, ask questions, complete your practice, and absorb the knowledge with sincerity.

निष्ठा

Nishtha · sincerity

Regular practice and respect for the text. The relationship is built on learning, not on a checkout.

अर्पणम्

Arpana · offering

Offer the Guru Dakshina agreed with the Acharya, monthly or per term, to support the teaching.

Why it matters

When the Acharya does not teach only for money, and the student does not pay only for a certificate, the relationship centres on learning and truth.
