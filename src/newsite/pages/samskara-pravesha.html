Leading the programme

Seven years in a Gurukula, for your child.

I studied for seven years, from 2018 to 2025, at Veda Vijnana Gurukulam in Bengaluru: Sanskrit grammar, Krishna Yajurveda and the Bhagavad Gita with its classical commentary. With children, my aim is simple: clean pronunciation, real understanding, and the quiet confidence a Gurukula gives.

More about the Acharya

Questions

Parents often ask.

Prefer classes just for your child? See the one-to-one pathway

Does my child need to know any Sanskrit already?

No. Level 1 starts from the very first sounds and letters. Children who already know a few shlokas will simply move a little faster.

Which language are classes taught in?

Sanskrit is taught with explanations in Kannada and English, so children from Kannada-medium and English-medium schools are equally comfortable.

How long is the programme?

Six levels of 24 live classes each: 144 classes over about 18 months. Each level takes about three months, and you can decide level by level.

What does a child need for class?

A laptop or tablet with a stable internet connection, a notebook and a pencil. A quiet corner at home helps a lot.

Why is the first batch called the Dhruva cohort?

Every batch is named after a young hero from our tradition and keeps that name for all 18 months. Dhruva was a boy who sat steady until he reached his goal.
