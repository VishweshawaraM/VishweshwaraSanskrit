The pathway

Six stages,
one steady road.

Months are a guide. Every child moves at their own pace, and moves on only when the last stage is theirs.

Level 1 · Beginner, from scratchAbout 5 to 7 months

Sounds, script and simple sentences

Stage I (Samskrita Pravesha) and Stage II (Vakya Nirmana). You learn to say every letter correctly, write Devanagari, and start speaking simple Sanskrit.

Vowels, consonants and their correct soundsDevanagari writing practiceWords for the body, food, objects and numbersIntroducing yourself in SanskritPronouns, gender and numberQuestion words and simple sentences
Level 2 · IntermediateAbout 6 to 8 months

How words change

Stage III (Shabda Rupa) and Stage IV (Dhatu Rupa). You see how nouns and verbs change their form, and why.

Noun forms: Rama, Rama (feminine), Phala, Hari, Guru, PitrPronouns: asmad, yushmad, tad, kimThe seven vibhaktis (cases)Common dhatus and their formsPresent, past, future and command (lat, lan, lrt, lot)
Level 3 · AdvancedAbout 7 to 9 months

Traditional grammar

Stage V (Vyakarana Pravesha) and Stage VI (Shastra Samskritam). Formal grammar, and then the Laghu Siddhanta Kaumudi, so you can open a text and read it yourself.

Sandhi: vowel, consonant and visargaSamasa: tatpurusha, karmadharaya, dvandva, bahuvrihiKaraka: how a sentence really worksLaghu Siddhanta Kaumudi, selected prakaranasReading shlokas, subhashitas and small granthas

Where your child arrives

Reading Sanskrit on their own. Words, then sentences, then stories, shlokas and subhashitas, and finally original texts. The aim is understanding, not memorising.
