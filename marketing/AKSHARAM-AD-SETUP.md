## 1. Campaign
- Objective: **Traffic** → Continue
- Campaign name: `Aksharam IME — Mac Sanskrit keyboard`
- Turn OFF "Advantage+ campaign budget" (we set the budget on the ad set) → Next

## 2. Ad set
- Name: `Sanskrit places — India + abroad`
- Conversion location: **Website**
- Performance goal: **Maximise number of landing page views**
- Budget: **Daily budget ₹200**
- Schedule: start **today**, **set an end date 10 days later**
- Audience → Locations: delete "India" (if there), then search and add each place below.
  Click a place → set radius **+25 km** (India towns +40 km).
  - India: Bengaluru · Tirupati · New Delhi · Varanasi · Ujjain · Puri · Haridwar · Jaipur · Mysuru · Udupi · Sringeri · Kalady · Nagpur · Veraval · Darbhanga · Pune

  - UK: Oxford · Cambridge · London · Leicester · Watford
  - Germany: Heidelberg · Hamburg · Leipzig · Berlin · Oppenau
  - Australia: Canberra · Sydney · Melbourne
- Age **18 – 55** · Gender **All**
- Detailed targeting (add as "suggestions"): **Yoga** · **Culture of India** · **MacBook** · **MacBook Pro**
- Placements: **Advantage+ placements** (on)
- If asked about the EU (Germany): beneficiary and payer = `Vishweshwara Sanskrit`
→ Next

## 3. Ad
- Name: `Aksharam — real people video`
- Identity: Facebook Page **Vishweshwara Sanskrit** + Instagram account **@vishweshwarasanskrit** (your page/IG)
- Format: **Single image or video** → Add media → **Add video** → upload **Aksharam-Ad-v2.mp4**
- Primary text:
  ```

  Aksharam IME — type in English, get perfect Devanagari in every app on your Mac: halant, conjuncts, even Vedic svaras.
  ```

- Headline: `Type Sanskrit on your Mac`
- Description: `₹99 one-time · lifetime licence`
- Call to action: **Learn more**
- Website URL: `https://vishweshwarasanskrit.com/aksharam`
- Turn OFF "Advantage+ creative" enhancements (keeps the video and text exactly as made)

## 4. Publish
Check the preview on **Instagram Reels** and **Feed** → **Publish**.
Meta reviews it (usually under 24 hours), then it starts.
